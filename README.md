## What I do

- 🛠️ **Builder.** I build the apps, and these days the automations too. I started with Android apps in Java, moved to Flutter, and shipped a hackathon MVP in Next.js in April 2026.
- ✍️ **Technical writer.** I write the tech down so it makes sense: 14 articles on my own blog, 30+ pieces on ERP systems at BigFix, and two AI newsletters that grew past 1,000 subscribers at Autogon AI.
- 🤝 **Technical sales.** I sell technical products by showing them working. I led product demos at Lendsqr and Autogon AI, and built the lead-scoring automations behind my own outreach.
- 🎤 **Community builder.** 400+ members across the communities I've led, and 1,000+ participants at the hackathons and model-build sprints I organised.
